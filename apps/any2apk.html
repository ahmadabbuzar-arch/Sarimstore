About Any2APK

Any2APK is a website to APK builder that turns a ZIP, HTML code or a live website URL into a signed Android app. Choose your source, enter the app name and package name, add your own app icon and an optional splash screen, then tap Build APK. You can pick the permissions your app needs, such as camera, microphone, location and vibration. Optional features include push notifications with Firebase, so you can send notifications to everyone who installed your app, and app updates, so users see an Update available message when you publish a newer version. You can also turn on ads to show a banner and an occasional full-screen ad. The History section keeps your recent builds saved on your device, where you can download the APK, open the build, send notifications, publish a new version or copy the update key.
