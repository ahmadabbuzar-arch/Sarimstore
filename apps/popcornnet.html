About PopcornNet

PopcornNet is a movie and series app for Android. Browse the Home, Movies and Series tabs, see featured titles with their year, rating and story summary, and search by title, people or genre. Open any title to see its details and a More Like This list of similar picks. The built-in player lets you choose the video quality (Auto, 4K, 1080p and more), switch the audio language, change the playback speed and watch in fullscreen. The interface is dark, clean and easy to use on a phone.
