About

Sarim Store & Sarim Productions

Sarim Store

Sarim Store is an independent app marketplace built to distribute apps outside of the usual app store ecosystem. It's not affiliated with, and doesn't imitate, any existing platform — it exists purely to host and share apps built by Sarim Productions directly with users.

Sarim Productions

Sarim Productions designs and builds independent Android applications with a focus on clean design and straightforward functionality. Every app listed here is made in-house, reviewed, and published directly to this store.

A note on installs

Apps downloaded here are distributed as APK files outside of any third-party store. You may need to allow installs from your browser or file manager the first time you install one.
